## Rules for every post
- **Link:** use the tagged link for each platform (below), so you can see which video brings people in.
  - TikTok: `facetpro.co.uk/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch`
  - Instagram: `facetpro.co.uk/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch`
  - Facebook: `facetpro.co.uk/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch`
- **TikTok AI label:** turn on "AI-generated content" when posting the **three polished videos** (they use AI house pictures). The 12 site recordings show the real website; their demo house is the AI illustration on the homepage, so turn the label on for clips 01, 02 and 12 too. TikTok requires a label on realistic AI images, and a label it adds itself can't be removed.
- **Never** write "quote" or "get quotes" until quote requests are switched on. Say "estimate".
- **Reply to every comment** in the first hour, even with one line. That's the cheapest reach there is.
