Fonts served from this directory
================================

geist-variable.woff2
  Geist, by Vercel. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/vercel/geist-font
  One variable file covering weights 300-600. Latin subset only.

instrument-serif-400.woff2
instrument-serif-400-italic.woff2
  Instrument Serif, by Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jean-Baptiste Morizot.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif
  Latin subset only.

Both licences permit redistribution and self-hosting. They are here rather
than on fonts.googleapis.com so that no visitor's IP address reaches a third
party just for loading a page — which matters most on the privacy notice,
whose purpose is to explain that we do not do that sort of thing.

The subsets were produced by Google Fonts and fetched from fonts.gstatic.com.
