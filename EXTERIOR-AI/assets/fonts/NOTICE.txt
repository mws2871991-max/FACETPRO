Fonts served from this directory
================================

geist-variable.woff2
  Geist, by Vercel. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/vercel/geist-font
  One variable file; the weight axis runs past 800 (measured 6 Oct 2026 —
  text widens at 600, 700 and 800 with font synthesis off). Latin subset only.

GeistMono-500.woff2
  Geist Mono, by Vercel. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/vercel/geist-font
  Weight 500, latin subset. The eyebrow labels on the ProFacet homepage.

instrument-serif-400.woff2
instrument-serif-400-italic.woff2
  Instrument Serif, by Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jean-Baptiste Morizot.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif
  Latin subset only.

jetbrains-mono-variable.woff2
  JetBrains Mono, by JetBrains. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono
  One variable file covering weights 400-500. Latin subset only.
  Used for measured values — money, areas, counts, references — because a
  monospace numeral reads as taken off a building, and a proportional one
  reads as chosen.

All four licences permit redistribution and self-hosting. They are here rather
than on fonts.googleapis.com so that no visitor's IP address reaches a third
party just for loading a page — which matters most on the privacy notice,
whose purpose is to explain that we do not do that sort of thing.

The subsets were produced by Google Fonts and fetched from fonts.gstatic.com.
