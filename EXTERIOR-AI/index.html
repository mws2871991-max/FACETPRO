Questions homeowners ask

Will someone call me?

⌄

Not unless you ask to be contacted. You can upload a photo, design your whole exterior and read the estimate without giving us a name, a number or an email. If you do want quotes, you choose when — and at most three installers ever receive your details.

How much do new windows cost in the UK?

⌄

It depends on how many windows you're replacing, how big they are and the style you choose. Facet Pro prices yours the way an installer would: each window by its size and style, the door by the leaf, plus access for anything upstairs, taking the old frames away, and VAT at 20%. You see every line. It's an estimate to plan with, not a fixed quote. The rates behind it are what these jobs actually settle at, from twenty-four years of selling them — not a list price nobody pays.

Do I need to measure my house first?

⌄

No. Start with a clear photo of the front of your home — Facet Pro uses the visible features and proportions in it to size the job. If anything looks wrong, correct it with one tap and the estimate updates.

Will you change my window frames in the picture?

⌄

Yes. Pick a style and a frame colour and the after picture is generated from your own photo — keeping your home's perspective, lighting and shadows, and showing the frames and door you chose where it can. Nothing is added, removed or resized. It's a realistic guide to help you choose, not a guarantee of the finished installation — and if the picture hasn't changed something you asked for, your estimate still prices what you chose.

What happens to my photo?

⌄

Your original photograph is never saved. It goes to the AI services that find the parts of your home and generate the picture, and it is not kept, published, sold, or used to train anybody's models. What we do keep: the picture we generate for you, the choices you made, and a fingerprint of your photo with the measurements taken from it — the fingerprint for seven days, so the same photo always gives you the same estimate. Full detail is in the privacy notice.

How long does it take?

⌄

Finding the windows, doors, roof and cladding takes about 30 seconds. Colour changes and prices update instantly. A full photorealistic render of your home can take up to a minute.

Can I still price cladding, render or a new roof?

⌄

Yes — that is rather the point. Windows, doors, walls, roofline and roof all price off the one photo you already uploaded. Most people start with the windows and add the rest once they can see what the whole job comes to. Conservatories are the exception: we show guide ranges by style to help you compare, because a conservatory's price turns on size, glazing, groundworks and access rather than on anything a photograph of the front can tell us.

Does Facet Pro do the work?

⌄

No. Facet Pro creates the design and the estimate. If you ask for quotes, it goes to installers who arrange a survey and quote properly. There is no obligation to go ahead.

The pricing problem

Two neighbours. Same street.
Same windows. Thousands apart.

Two similar homes can end up paying very different prices for similar work. Sometimes that is timing. Sometimes it is negotiation. Mostly it is how the sales process unfolds — and none of it is anything to do with the windows.

Facet Pro was built by somebody who sold these jobs out of people’s front rooms for twenty-four years. Long enough to know what the work really costs, and how often two similar houses end up paying very different amounts for it. This puts that in the homeowner’s hands before the sales process starts.

What can change your final price?

Facet Pro gives you a planning estimate, not a fixed contract quote. The final price can change after a survey, because of things a photograph cannot always show:

  • • The condition of the property
  • • Access and installation requirements
  • • Hidden or unexpected work
  • • Final measurements — we are still calibrating how we size a house from a photograph against surveyed properties
  • • Your final product specification

That is why we show a realistic range rather than pretending one number is guaranteed. If a figure looks wrong for your home, tell us when you save your design.

How can one photo be enough? Detection, measurement and where it is still being calibrated

Accuracy

How can one photo be enough?

A photo contains more information than you might think. Facet Pro uses visible features, proportions and familiar dimensions to build an initial understanding of your home’s exterior — a standard UK front door is very close to 1.98 m, and it is in almost every photograph of a British home, so it gives us the scale of everything around it. Where more detail is needed, you can check or correct it before your estimate is calculated.

Scale from the door

A UK front door is a known height. The elevation is measured against it — no reference object, no tape measure, no visit.

Whole-house inference

One elevation is visible; a quote covers the house. Front measurements scale to the whole property by house type.

Stated uncertainty

Every estimate carries a range and says which method produced it. A number without its error bars is a guess.
