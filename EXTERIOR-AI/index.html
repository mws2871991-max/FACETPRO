What your estimate actually includes

Most online calculators give you one number with nothing behind it. Facet Pro shows the whole build-up, the way an installer would price it.

Cladding

Material and the labour to fit it, priced per square metre.

Roof

Tiles or slate plus fitting, sized from your roof area.

Fascia, soffit & guttering

Priced by the metre of roofline, materials and labour.

Scaffolding

The bit that surprises people. It's in your figure from the start.

Waste allowance

Offcuts and breakages, so the number holds up on site.

VAT at 20%

Shown separately, never hidden in the headline price.

Facet Pro gives you a planning estimate, not a fixed quote. Your final price depends on the condition of your walls and roof, access, and what an installer finds when they survey your home.

Questions homeowners ask

How much does it cost to re-clad a house in the UK?

⌄

It depends on the size of your home and the materials you choose. Facet Pro builds your figure the way an installer would: materials plus labour for the cladding, roof and trim, then scaffolding, a waste allowance and VAT at 20%. You see every line, so you know what you're looking at. It's an estimate to plan with, not a fixed quote.

Do I need to measure my house first?

⌄

No. Facet Pro starts from a typical UK home footprint so you get a figure straight away. If you happen to know your wall area in square metres, or the length of your fascia and guttering run, type them in and the estimate is recalculated around your actual house.

Is the after picture really my house?

⌄

Yes. The render is generated from the photo you upload, keeping your home's perspective, lighting and shadows and changing only the cladding, trim colour and roof material. It's a realistic guide to help you choose, not a guarantee of the finished installation.

What happens to my photo?

⌄

Your photo is sent to the AI services that analyse your home and create the render. It isn't saved to our records and it isn't published anywhere. Only your design choices, your estimate and the contact details you type in are kept, so we can get back to you.

How long does it take?

⌄

Finding the windows, doors, roof and cladding takes a few seconds. Colour changes and prices update instantly. A full photorealistic render of your home can take up to a minute.

Does Facet Pro do the work?

⌄

Facet Pro creates the design and the estimate. When you save your design it goes to installers who can quote for the job properly and arrange a survey. There's no obligation to go ahead.
