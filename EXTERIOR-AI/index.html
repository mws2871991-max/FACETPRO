Proprietary technology

What happens to your photograph

A photograph is not a measurement. Turning one into a price means working out how big the building actually is, and there is only one object of known size in almost every picture of a British home: the front door. It is 1.98 m. Everything else follows from it.

Component detection

Every window, door, wall and roof located as a distinct element, with a confidence score attached to each.

Scale from the door

A UK front door is a known height. The elevation is measured against it — no reference object, no tape measure, no visit.

Whole-house inference

One elevation is visible; a quote covers the house. Front measurements scale to the whole property by house type.

Pricing engine

Areas and counts become itemised costs across six trades, computed on the server so the figure cannot be tampered with.

Visualisation

Your actual house re-finished photorealistically — the perspective, shadows and openings held exactly as they were.

Stated uncertainty

Every estimate carries a range and says which method produced it. A number without its error bars is a guess.

Facet Pro combines industry-leading AI models with its own proprietary measurement, pricing and renovation intelligence. The vision and image-generation models are licensed; the measurement method, the whole-house inference, the pricing engine and the rate catalogue behind it are ours.

Why this is hard to copy

The models can be rented by anybody. What cannot be rented is knowing what a job settles at — not the list price, but the number it actually goes out at once the discounting is done. That came from twenty-four years of selling these in people’s houses, and it is what the whole estimate rests on. A competitor with the same models and a published rate card produces confident figures that are wrong, and finds out at survey.

The second half is supply. Installers who will pay for leads have to be signed, and they sign with somebody they already know. Both of ours were.

And it compounds. The system is built so that every property analysed and every job completed feeds back into the pricing intelligence, the measurement accuracy and the picture of what people are actually renovating — so the estimates get sharper as the platform grows, and the gap in front of anybody starting from scratch widens.

Why this exists

Built by somebody who sold these for twenty-four years

Front rooms, on weeknights, for a national installer. Long enough to know exactly what a job settles at — and exactly why two identical houses on the same street end up paying wildly different amounts for the same work.

It is not the product that varies. It is how long somebody is willing to sit there. Plenty of people do not want that evening, and should not have to have it in order to be charged a fair price.

Facet Pro is that knowledge written down: what these jobs actually cost, offered to everybody at the same number, from one photograph, without anybody coming to the house.

What it doesn’t know yet

This is a planning estimate, not a fixed quote — built to give you a realistic cost while you are still deciding. Your final price depends on the condition of your walls and roof, access, and what an installer finds on survey.

Two things we are still sharpening, and would rather say than not: window rates are indicative until our supplier rate card lands, and we are still calibrating how we measure a house from a photograph against surveyed properties. If a figure looks wrong for your home, tell us when you save your design — that is exactly what we are trying to get right.

Questions homeowners ask

How much do new windows cost in the UK?

⌄

It depends on how many windows you're replacing, how big they are and the style you choose. Facet Pro prices yours the way an installer would: each window by its size and style, the door by the leaf, plus access for anything upstairs, taking the old frames away, and VAT at 20%. You see every line. It's an estimate to plan with, not a fixed quote — and while we finish sourcing our supplier rates, treat the window figures as an early guide.

Do I need to measure my house first?

⌄

No. We measure your windows from the photo, using your front door as the reference — a UK front door is a standard height, so it tells us the scale of everything else. If we've counted wrong, correct it with one tap and the estimate updates. You can also type in your wall area or your guttering run if you happen to know them.

Will you change my window frames in the picture?

⌄

Yes. Pick a style and a frame colour and the after picture is generated from your own photo — keeping your home's perspective, lighting and shadows, and changing the frames and door to what you chose. Nothing is added, removed or resized. It's a realistic guide to help you choose, not a guarantee of the finished installation.

What happens to my photo?

⌄

Your photo is sent to the AI services that analyse your home and create the render. It isn't saved to our records and it isn't published anywhere. Only your design choices, your estimate and the contact details you type in are kept, so we can get back to you.

How long does it take?

⌄

Finding the windows, doors, roof and cladding takes a few seconds. Colour changes and prices update instantly. A full photorealistic render of your home can take up to a minute.

Can I still price cladding, render or a new roof?

⌄

Yes — that is rather the point. Windows, doors, walls, roofline, roof and a conservatory all price off the one photo you already uploaded. Most people start with the windows and add the rest once they can see what the whole job comes to.

Does Facet Pro do the work?

⌄

Facet Pro creates the design and the estimate. When you save your design it goes to installers who can quote for the job properly and arrange a survey. There's no obligation to go ahead.
