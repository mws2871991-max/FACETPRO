The Facet Pro price promise

Version 1.0

For the job you chose, your installer’s quote will be within your Facet Pro range, and never more than 13% above it. If the job changes at survey, any difference is explained to you in writing.

1. Which quotes it covers

Quotes from the installers you asked for quotes through Facet Pro, for the enquiry you sent us. Your confirmation email shows your reference, your Facet Pro range and the highest figure the promise allows for each part of the job.

2. What “the job you chose” means

The products, colours, number and type of windows and doors, and the work you chose on Facet Pro, as they are listed in your confirmation email.

3. The limit

Your estimate is worked out for each part of the job: windows and doors, and walls, roof and roofline. For each part, the installer’s quote for the job you chose will be within your Facet Pro range and no more than 13% above the top of it. For example, if your range for windows is £7,770 to £14,128, the most the promise allows for those windows is £15,965. A quote below your range is fine.

4. When the job changes at survey

A photograph cannot show everything. If the survey finds the job is different, the installer may quote above the limit, but must explain every difference to you in writing. The reasons are:

  • more, or larger, windows or doors than your estimate included;
  • a different product, colour or extra that you choose at the survey;
  • the condition of the property, such as rot or structural work;
  • access or scaffolding beyond the usual.

5. What it is not

It is not a fixed price, a guarantee of your final price or a price match. Your Facet Pro figure is still a planning estimate, as section 4 of our terms explains. You never have to accept a quote, and your contract for any work is with the installer, not with us (section 8 of our terms).

6. If an installer does not keep it

Email hello@facetpro.co.uk with your reference and the quote. We will take it up with the installer, ask them to bring the quote within the limit or explain it in writing, and an installer who does not keep the promise can be removed from the installers we send enquiries to.

7. Who stands behind it

The installers we send enquiries to have agreed to this promise in writing with Facet Pro.

8. Changes

We may change or end the promise for future enquiries. Any change does not affect an enquiry you have already sent us.
