Your Facet Pro price is a planning estimate — not a quotation.

It gives you a useful starting point for understanding what your project could cost. An installer will confirm the final specification and price after surveying the property.

Where does the estimate come from?

We combine the information available from your property and selected specification with UK pricing data and typical project costs to produce a realistic price range. It is designed to help you plan — not replace an installer survey.

Questions, answered.

The detail lives in our privacy policy and how we price. Facet Pro is free for homeowners; installers pay us a fee only when you choose to request quotes.

Is the Facet Pro price a quotation?

No. It is a planning estimate. The final price is confirmed by an installer after reviewing the property and specification.

Do I have to speak to an installer?

No. You can explore, price and save your design without speaking to anyone.

Will installers contact me automatically?

No. You decide whether to request quotes.

How many installers will receive my details?

Up to three, and only if you request quotes.

Do I need to measure my property?

No. We size it from your photo.

What happens to my photo?

Your original photo is deleted immediately after processing. We keep a secure fingerprint for 7 days to maintain estimate consistency, and store your generated designs for 6 months so you can return to your project. The detail is in our privacy policy.

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a realistic planning range, not a guaranteed final price. It is based on what we can see in your photo and the options you choose.
